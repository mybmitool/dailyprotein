Why I Stopped Logging My Sunday Ice Cream (And Still Hit My Macros)

By Derek Armstrong • June 06, 2026 • 4 min read
Look, I have a confession. I'm obsessive. I track everything. My protein, my carbs, my fat, my fiber, my water intake. I have a spreadsheet for my spreadsheet. My roommate calls me “the robot.” He's not wrong. But every Sunday, I eat ice cream. A whole pint. Ben & Jerry's. Phish Food. I don't log it. I don't measure it. I just eat it. And I still hit my macros for the week. The Macro Split Planner can help you build flexibility into your own plan.

 Here's the thing that took me years to learn. Total weekly protein matters more than daily perfection. If you hit your target six days out of seven, one day of ice cream doesn't break the system. Your muscles don't reset at midnight. They average over time. I used to be the guy who weighed his chicken breast on a food scale. Every meal. Every gram. I'd log everything in MyMacros+. If I went over my fat by 5 grams, I'd do extra cardio. That's not dedication. That's disordered. The Protein Intake Calculator can help you set a realistic target so you don't have to be perfect.

 I remember the exact moment I stopped obsessing. It was a Sunday night. My roommate had bought two pints of ice cream. He handed me one. I opened my app to log it. Then I closed the app. I just ate it. No tracking. No guilt. No extra cardio the next day. The world didn't end. My abs didn't disappear. I still hit 180 grams of protein that day because I ate a normal breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The ice cream was extra. It didn't replace anything. It was just… pleasure. Listen, the research on flexible dieting is clear. A 2022 study compared two groups of lifters on the same calorie and protein targets. One group ate a “clean” diet – chicken, rice, broccoli. The other group ate the same macros but with 20% of calories from “fun” foods – cookies, chips, ice cream. After 12 weeks, both groups had the same body composition changes. Same fat loss. Same muscle gain. The clean group was more miserable. The Meal Protein Tracker can help you focus on protein first, then enjoy the rest.

 Here's my rule. Six days a week, I hit my protein target. 180 grams. Spread across four meals. I eat chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, whey, cottage cheese, beef, fish. I don't enjoy every meal. Some are boring. That's fine. It's fuel. One day a week, Sunday, I don't track. I still eat protein, but I don't measure or log. I eat ice cream. I eat pizza. I have a beer. I live my life. The math works because 6/7 is 85%. 85% compliance is enough. The difference between 85% and 100% is not meaningful for body composition. It is meaningful for sanity. I have clients who tell me “I can't stick to a diet.” I ask them why. They say “because I can't eat ice cream.” I tell them to eat the ice cream. Just hit your protein first. One client, a woman named Jess, was eating 120 grams of protein a day and 40 grams of fat from “clean” sources. She was miserable. She craved chocolate every night. She'd binge on weekends. She thought she had no willpower. I told her to eat a chocolate bar every day. Just log it. She did. She stopped binging. Her body fat went down. Her mood improved. The difference wasn't willpower. It was permission. So here's my challenge to you. Pick one meal per week. One. Don't log it. Don't measure it. Just eat it. Enjoy it. Then go back to your plan. See what happens. I bet you'll still make progress. The Macro Split Planner is free. The Protein Intake Calculator is free. The Meal Protein Tracker is free. Your sanity is also free. Don't trade it for an extra 2 grams of protein. Sunday is for ice cream. I stand by that.
