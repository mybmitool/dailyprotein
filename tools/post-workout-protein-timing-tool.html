How This Tool Works

This tool debunks the "anabolic window" myth and generates an evidence-based protein timing protocol. It analyzes your wake time, workout time, last pre-workout meal, and bedtime to distribute protein optimally across your day. The timeline visualization shows exactly when to eat for maximum muscle protein synthesis pulses.

Fasted training: If you train without eating for 6+ hours, the tool prioritizes faster post-workout protein (within 1 hour). If you ate 1-2 hours before training, the window extends to 2-3 hours because amino acids are still circulating. Total daily protein matters more than timing — but timing helps consistency.

Pre-sleep casein: The protocol always includes a pre-sleep protein source (cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, or casein powder) 30-60 minutes before bed. Studies show 30-40g casein before sleep increases overnight muscle protein synthesis by ~22% without affecting fat metabolism.
