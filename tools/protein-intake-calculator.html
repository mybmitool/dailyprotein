How This Calculator Works

This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation combined with activity multipliers and goal-specific adjustments. For muscle gain, we apply a 1.6-2.2 g/kg multiplier. For fat loss (cutting), we increase protein to 1.8-2.4 g/kg to preserve lean mass. If you enter body fat percentage, we calculate lean body mass and base protein targets on that instead of total weight — more accurate for people with higher body fat.

The science: The International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN) position stand recommends 1.4-2.0 g/kg for most athletes, with higher intakes (up to 2.4 g/kg) during calorie deficits. This calculator implements those ranges with practical rounding.

Your data stays private. All calculations are stored in your browser's localStorage. We never see your weight, body fat, or goals. Clear your browser cache to delete all history.
