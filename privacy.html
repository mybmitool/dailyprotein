Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal data through our calculators. All calculations, meal logs, and tracking data are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. We never see your weight, body fat percentage, or food logs.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We use basic analytics to understand site traffic patterns. This may include anonymous IP addresses, browser types, and page views. No personally identifiable information is collected.

3. Third-Party Services

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. These third parties may use cookies to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ad Settings.

4. Data Security

Since all calculation data stays in your browser, you control your data. Clearing your browser cache will remove all stored calculations and meal logs.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email privacy@dailyprotein.net.
